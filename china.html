Chapter I · the dark half

Shanghai

Shanghai, China · ages 6–16

  • 01

    Out of the blue

    When I was 6, my family decided to abruptly move to China. Out of the blue, no warning at all. At the time, I didn’t speak a lick of Mandarin at all. So while I looked the part, all it took was one short conversation here or a quick remark there for everyone to see through my façade. I was then the outsider, “the American”.

  • 龙
    Fun fact

    龙 (n.)

    The Chinese character for dragon.

    My surname literally means dragon.

  • 02

    Back to where it began

    Cut to 10 years later, and I’d eventually assimilated well enough to life in China. But just as I began getting comfortable, I was hit with a move again. Once again, just as out of the blue as 10 years ago, only this time it was back to where it all began.
