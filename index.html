I was born and raised in Lower Merion, a quiet suburb just outside Philadelphia. Foreign at Home

This time, language wasn't the problem — my English was perfectly fine. But fluency isn't the same as fitting in. I didn't know the references. I didn't know the slang. I was ostracized all over again — not for being too American this time, but for not being American enough.
