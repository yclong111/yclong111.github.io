My Upbringing

My childhood was far from static. Born and raised in the United States, I moved during two crucial moments of my life: first, when I was 6, all the way to Shanghai, China. Then a second time when I was 16, this time all the way back to the States. And while it definitely was hectic, those experiences also taught me some pretty unique cross-cultural perspectives early on in life.
