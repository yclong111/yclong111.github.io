My Upbringing

The US

Born and raised in Lower Merion, PA in the US. Then, when I was 16, as a rising junior, I moved all the way back to the US. Once again, I had to give up everything I had built in the last 10 years. Even though I spoke English perfectly fine, I still wasn’t fully “American.” I wouldn’t understand the specific references, slang, etc. Once again, I was ostracized, this time for not being American enough.

China

When I was 6, I moved to Shanghai, China abruptly, despite not knowing any Mandarin at all. While I looked the part, all it took was one quick conversation for people to see through my facade that I was American.
