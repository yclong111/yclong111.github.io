Philadelphia

Lower Merion, Pennsylvania · ages 0–6, then 16 to now

  • 01

    Lower Merion

    I was born and raised right outside Philadelphia in Lower Merion, Pennsylvania. If you’re into basketball, you might know it because Kobe went there for high school.

  • 02

    Only American

    Despite being born to two hard-working, incredibly loving Chinese immigrant parents, I always struggled to identify with my ethnic roots. My parents purposefully named me after my Chinese name, 龙毅诚, in hopes that I would resonate more with my Chinese side, but honestly I just hated how a red line would always appear under my name when my teachers typed it in their computer. Rather, I always just viewed myself as American, and only American.

Little did I know, this would all soon change.
