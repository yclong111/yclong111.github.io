Philadelphia

Lower Merion, Pennsylvania · ages 0–6, then 16 to now

  • 01

    Lower Merion

    I was born and raised right outside Philadelphia in Lower Merion, Pennsylvania. If you’re into basketball, you might know it because Kobe went there for high school.

  • 02

    Only American

    I was born to two hard-working and incredibly loving Chinese immigrants. My parents had purposefully given me an odd name, Yicheng, based after my Chinese name 龙毅诚, in hopes that I would resonate more with my Chinese side. Instead, I had always found it hard to identify with my ethnic roots, and I hated how a red line would always appear under my name when my teachers typed it in their computer.

    I read Harry Potter, not Journey to the West. My favorite food was fried chicken from KFC, not General Tso’s. My favorite TV show was Wild Kratts, and honestly to this day, I still don’t know a single Chinese TV show. I didn’t even speak Mandarin. Long story short, I really had always just viewed myself as American, and only American.

Little did I know, this would all soon change.
